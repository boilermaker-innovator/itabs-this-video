## The big idea

**AI is the new shopfront.**

People now ask AI assistants where to buy, who to call and what to use. If AI can read you and trusts you, it names you. If it can't, you're invisible, however good your Google ranking is.

## Key points

1. **ChatGPT can beat Google.** For Manager 11, ChatGPT sent more signups than Google search, with zero ad spend.
2. **Ask the buying questions every month.** Track the same questions your customers ask AI, and see whether you're named.
3. **Check the tool before you trust it.** Tools can be wrong, so check their numbers against Google's own data.
4. **Fix, don't just report.** Claude finds the problem and ships the fix the same day.

## How Claude runs the SEO: six steps

1. **Pull the data.** Scripts Claude wrote pull Search Console, GA4, Google Ads, Meta and SEMrush.
2. **Read what APIs can't.** Claude in Chrome reads Google's AI report, Shopify and Clarity.
3. **Track AI answers.** The same buying questions, asked every month.
4. **Cross-check.** Tool numbers get checked against Google's own data first.
5. **Fix, don't just report.** The FAT Agent audits the site and Claude ships the fix that day.
6. **Write it up.** A 42-slide monthly report, built in a day.

> **Why "check the tool" matters:** on 5 November 2026, SEMrush said a ratchet-strap page ranked #1. Google's own data said 9 to 11. A live check said #7. So Claude checks the tool's number before it goes in the report.

> **What the FAT Agent is:** a free, open-source SEO and security auditor for Claude Code. It checks Google spam policies, SEO, security, accessibility and speed, then fixes what it finds. fatagent.app · GitHub: spruikco/fat-agent-skill

## Real numbers: an Adelaide caravan dealer

Budget halved, 9 keywords paused, 7 negative keywords added, bidding switched to maximise conversions. **October: 128 leads for $903.**

| Measure | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Cost per lead, August | $20.50 |
| Cost per lead, September | $8.54 |
| Cost per lead, October | $7.05 |
| Google clicks vs October 2025 | +128% |
| Average position | 9.2, up from 19.3 |
| Buying questions where Google's AI names them | 13 of 15 |

> **Built paused:** Python scripts create every campaign paused, and nothing goes live without a human. Claude flags search terms that spend money with zero conversions and writes the negative keywords weekly. A conversion means a real enquiry.

## How to apply this to your own business

Copy everything in the box below and paste it into your own AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, whatever you use).

```text
I watched Rye Smith's talk from Spruik on getting found by AI and running
SEO with Claude. The idea: AI is the new shopfront. People ask ChatGPT,
Perplexity and Google AI where to buy, and the businesses AI can read and
trust get named.

His main points:
1. ChatGPT can beat Google as a source of customers.
2. Ask the same buying questions every month and check if you're named.
3. Check a tool's numbers against Google's own data before trusting them.
4. Fix, don't just report: find the problem and fix it the same day.

Help me apply this to my own business, one step at a time:

1. First ask me about my business: what I sell, who my customers are,
   where I'm based, and my website address if I have one.
2. Help me write 3 to 5 questions my customers would ask an AI assistant
   when looking for what I sell.
3. Tell me how to ask those questions in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI,
   and how to record whether I'm named, cited or first.
4. Based on what I find, suggest the one simplest fix that would make my
   business easier for AI to read and recommend.
5. Give me a short monthly checklist so I can track progress.
6. Then stop and ask if I want to go further.

Keep explanations short and plain. Ask before assuming anything about my business.
```
